A photo is all it takes

No searching food databases, no typing. Three steps from plate to log.

1

Snap or pick a photo

Use the camera or choose a photo from your library.

2

AI estimates the nutrition

It lists each food it sees with a portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat, usually in a few seconds.

3

Track your day

The meal lands on your dashboard and counts toward your daily calories and macros.

Your day, at a glance

The dashboard shows what's left for the day, and you can switch to what you've eaten with a tap.

  • A week strip to look back at earlier days
  • A streak for every day in a row you log a meal
