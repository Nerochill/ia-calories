1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how [Company Legal Name] ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the IA Calories mobile app for iOS and Android and the online services that support it (together, the "Service"). 2Information we collect

Information from your sign-in

You sign in with your Apple or Google account, through our authentication provider, Clerk. Depending on what you allow Apple or Google to share, we receive your name, email address and profile picture, along with an account identifier. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead of your real email. We never receive your Apple or Google password. We store your email address in our database, linked to your account.

Information you give us during onboarding

  • Body and goals: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal (lose, maintain or gain weight) and diet preference (none, vegetarian, vegan, keto or high protein).
  • Preferences: unit system (metric or imperial) and language (English or Spanish).
  • Derived information: your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat targets, which are generated from your answers. We store your age as an approximate birth date so that your age stays current over time.

Meal photos and your food log

  • Photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library. Before uploading, the app shrinks each photo to about 1024 pixels wide and saves it as a new JPEG.
  • Meal records: the date and time you logged each meal, and the AI's results: the meal name, each food item with its portion, weight, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and a confidence score, plus the meal totals. We also keep the AI's full response and the name of the AI model that produced it, and whether the analysis succeeded or failed.

Information collected automatically

  • Time zone: the app reads your device's time zone when you finish onboarding. We use it to decide which day a meal belongs to and when your daily scan limit resets. We do not collect your location.
  • Diagnostics: we use Sentry to find and fix crashes and errors. The app sends Sentry error reports and app logs tagged with your account identifier, but not your name or email. Logs include events such as sign-ins, meal submissions and their timing, meal analysis results (number of items and total calories), the goal, diet and activity level you chose and the calorie target generated for you, and failed requests. Reports also include technical information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system and app version. We don't send your height, weight, age or photos to Sentry.

Feedback you send us

If you use Profile → Send feedback, we receive your message along with your email address (prefilled, and you can clear it) and any image you choose to attach. Your name from your account may also be included. Feedback is collected through Sentry.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your sign-in session in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). While you go through onboarding, it saves your answers on your device so you don't lose them. They're cleared when you finish onboarding, or when you delete your account.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising identifiers or payment information. We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3How we use your information

  • To provide the Service: creating and signing in to your account, generating your daily targets, analyzing your meal photos, and showing your food log and progress.
  • To enforce limits and protect the Service: applying the daily scan limit, checking that each uploaded photo belongs to your account, and preventing abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve reliability: investigating crashes, failed scans and slow requests.
  • To communicate with you: replying to feedback and support requests, and telling you about important changes to the Service or our policies.
  • To meet legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't use your photos or meal data to train AI models.

4AI processing

The Service uses OpenAI's API in two places:

  • Meal analysis: OpenAI receives a link to a resized copy of your meal photo (about 1024 pixels wide) and your app language, and returns the foods and nutrition estimates.
  • Daily targets: OpenAI receives your sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal and diet preference, and returns your daily targets.

We don't send your name, email address or account identifier to OpenAI. OpenAI processes this data as our service provider under its API terms. 10How we protect your information

Here are the safeguards we use:

  • Data travels between the app, our servers and our providers over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Every request to our servers must carry a valid sign-in session, which our servers verify.
  • Photo uploads use one-time signed permissions that expire after 10 minutes, and our servers check that each photo was uploaded to the uploading user's own folder.
  • Secret keys for our providers are kept on our servers, never in the app.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your device's secure storage.

About photo links: to show your photos in the app and let the AI analyze them, each photo is served from a long, hard-to-guess web address. These addresses aren't published anywhere, but anyone who has one could view the photo without signing in. Avoid taking meal photos that include people, documents or anything else you wouldn't want to share.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
